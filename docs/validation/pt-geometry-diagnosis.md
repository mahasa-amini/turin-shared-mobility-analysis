## Route 35 before and after dissolve

A focused diagnostic extracted the existing `make_shape_lines` function through AST without module startup effects. It selected route `35U` shapes from the same GTFS trips, applied the same projection, clip, and dissolve operations, and retained the intermediate WKB. All geometries in both environments were valid.

| Stage | Features (both) | Components original/tested | Total length original/tested (m) | Maximum Hausdorff distance (m) | Sum of exact symmetric-difference lengths (m) |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|---:|
| projected | 6 | 6 / 6 | 59893.959236250 / 59893.959236247 | 1.31708901597e-09 | 53893.160253418 |
| clipped | 5 | 32 / 32 | 31486.011849127 / 31486.011849127 | 1.31708901597e-09 | 52865.663169071 |
| dissolved | 1 | 131 / 145 | 20061.359308699 / 21150.098369166 | 1.31708901597e-09 | 0.000000000 |

Projected/clipped symmetric-difference lengths are summed per corresponding shape. Nanometre coordinate changes can make nearly coincident line segments distinct under an exact set operation, producing large symmetric-difference lengths even when lengths and Hausdorff distances nearly agree. The dissolved geometries have zero symmetric-difference length, but different stored segmentation and accumulated line length: **1,088.739060 m** difference. This is a representation/noding difference in overlapping linework, not a kilometre-scale displacement of the network footprint. It is still consequential for geometry-length or component-count measures and was not waived as harmless.

The projection version change introduces approximately `1e-9` m coordinate differences before clipping. Clipping and dissolving overlapping shapes amplify these into differences in multipart representation and accumulated length. The original environment's route-only diagnostic yielded 131 parts/20,061.359309 m, agreeing with the reference; the initially tested environment yielded 145 parts/21,150.098369 m. Most importantly, the controlled PyProj-only full preparation run restored agreement for every network layer, establishing the bundled projection-runtime version as the cause in this experiment. The experiment does not separate individual internal changes within the PROJ bundle.

## Resolution and downstream relevance

Pin `pyproj==3.7.2` explicitly and in the tested platform lock. This preserves the reference-generating projection behavior without changing clipping, dissolve, scientific logic, or tolerances. The resolved package set was checked with `pip check`, all nine environment smoke checks, and a non-mutating lock dry run.

Source inspection shows that PT proximity reads official stop/boundary layers, and route compatibility reconstructs ordered stop patterns from GTFS. Neither computes its classification from the dissolved `pt_routes_urban` layer. The final PT GIS preparation reads the per-shape `pt_route_shapes_urban` layer. Thus the observed dissolved-length discrepancy does not directly enter proximity or ordered-route classifications; effect on complete outputs is verified by subsequent reproduction checks rather than assumed from this inspection. The later GTFS remains a spatial/topological reference, not verified 2024 service.
